# The Orch OR Chain

An interactive walkthrough of the Penrose–Hameroff Orchestrated Objective Reduction theory,
in the narrowest reading current data will bear, with every link in the causal chain labelled
by what actually supports it.

**→ [Read it](https://ejhong.github.io/orch-or/)**

## Why

Most explanations of this theory present every step with the same confidence. In fact some
links are measured in vivo, several are in vitro only, one has been experimentally excluded,
and two are not testable with any instrument currently available. Reading it as a chain of
mixed-strength claims is the only way to see where the real work is.

## What's in it

- **A log-time axis** spanning the fifteen orders of magnitude the theory invokes, from
  Tegmark's femtosecond decoherence estimate to behavioural report, with the contested gap
  shaded.
- **A scrubber** through one conscious moment, eight stages, on the selection reading:
  classical preparation → superposition → E_G accumulation → threshold → collapse →
  conformational outcome → classical signalling → gain change and routing.
- **An evidence ledger** — the full chain, each link tagged *measured* / *in vitro only* /
  *contested* / *theoretical* / *excluded by experiment* / *not currently testable*.
- **An anesthesia comparison** showing where the three live accounts make different
  predictions, and where they make the same one.
- **Fit with the 2026 primate data** (Hesse et al.), both the parts it helps and the parts
  it doesn't.
- **Three proposed tests**, with what each can and cannot settle.

## A note on the reading presented

The "selection" framing — in which nothing informational is held in superposition, and the
collapse event selects among classically prepared alternatives — is a narrowing of the
published theory, not its authors' own formulation. It buys immunity to the no-cloning
argument against quantum agency and costs the entanglement account of unified experience.
Where it departs from Hameroff and Penrose, the evidence ledger says so.
